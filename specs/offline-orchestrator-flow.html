State-machine rules & methodology

Known errors always resolve to INVALID. Otherwise, unresolved applicable settings or missing freeze confirmation resolve to DRAFT_INCOMPLETE. Complete frozen settings with pending evidence resolve to EVIDENCE_UNCHECKED. Only all passing gates grant READY_FOR_OFFLINE_EXECUTION. Changing inputs revokes prior evidence.

Median PF ≥ 1.25 and median Sharpe ≥ 1.0 are aggregate research gates. Every seed must also have drawdown ≤ 10%, at least 30 trades, and positive net expectancy. Missing, failed or undefined RL metrics fail closed. A hash mismatch prevents every training lane from starting.
